! YouTube Comment Bots are currently applying the following strategy:
! Certain Bots without explicit profile pictures are copying comments with small modifications (e.g. adding a dot)
! These accounts don't have any content, and they have no links to sketchy websites.
! Instead, they link other bot accounts (with very explicit profile pictures) in the "recommended channels" section.
! This second category of bots then links to the sketchy websites.
! To allow viewing the first type of bots (and reporting them) without having to view the explicit profile pictures of the
! second type of bots, the following filter blocks all profile pictures of "recommended channels"
www.youtube.com##yt-img-shadow.ytd-grid-channel-renderer > img
